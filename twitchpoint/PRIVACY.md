# Privacy policy: Twitchpoint for Stream Deck

_Last updated: 2026-11-25_

The Twitchpoint Stream Deck plugin has no analytics, telemetry or ads, and it doesn't send your data to the developer or anyone else.

**Network connections.** It connects only to the Twitchpoint server URL you enter (by default `http://127.0.0.1:8080` on your own PC) and reads its status. It never contacts Twitch or any other service itself, and it holds no Twitch login.

**What it stores.** The server URL and refresh intervals, in Stream Deck's plugin settings on your computer.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the plugin from Stream Deck deletes everything it stored. Twitchpoint itself keeps its own data and Twitch login. See its documentation.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/FadingToJosh/streamdeck-plugins/issues
