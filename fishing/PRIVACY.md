# Privacy policy: Fishing for Stream Deck

_Last updated: 2026-11-25_

Fishing collects no personal data, has no analytics, telemetry or ads, and makes no network connections.

**What it stores.** Your catches, sizes and personal bests, in Stream Deck's plugin settings on your computer.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the plugin from Stream Deck deletes everything it stored.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/FadingToJosh/streamdeck-plugins/issues
